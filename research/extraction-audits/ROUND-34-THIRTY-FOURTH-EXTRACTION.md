## Item and mechanic notes

- The eight-item King Kai loot group preserves the exact receipt: one mutated Overlord soul, the Godslayer sword, five Immortal King armor pieces and two Moon God Orb fragments. The Ch754 loot panel ends with an ellipsis, so omitted items are not reconstructed.
- The 29-challenger inventory handover is recorded separately from the later 78 Silver, 61 Gold and 17 Black Jar opening. The opening output stays pooled; the two batches are not added together as if they were separate jar openings.
- The 17 Black Jars yield one unknown-quality Holy War Broken Blade among other exact printed outputs. The relic's source panel says it is a main advancement material for a Knight reaching Legendary or can provide 100 Divine Power before disappearing when exhausted.
- The poison core's description is partially ellipsized in the source. Only the printed 30% permanent maximum-health reduction, 60-second 蚀骨之毒 status and the stated Legendary-advancement role are retained. Its use is not treated as a universal guarantee of breakthrough.
- Chapter 757 shows three fruits in inventory while the current Ch755 Black Jar batch produced two. An earlier fruit receipt exists; the stock is not treated as a new three-fruit drop.
- Chapter 757 uses both 不灭战意 and 不灭之王 for the same five-piece bonus. Chapter 756's recipient wording and Chapter 761's profession labels are also recorded as unresolved source conflicts.
