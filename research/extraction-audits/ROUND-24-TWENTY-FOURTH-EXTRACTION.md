The seller `曹星` offered `冰原巨犀肉（传奇）` on the four-region market, **50,000 portions** stocked, maximum **2 portions per buyer**.

**Alternative** one-portion payment options and actually credited inventory:

| Accepted payment mode | Material units per portion | Actual credited quantity | Implied filled portions |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Wood | 20,000 | 314,000,000 | 15,700 |
| Stone | 10,000 | 154,000,000 | 15,400 |
