## FX31-0005 — Unfinished Legendary stasis mana obelisk: placement and shield

The inspected `未完成的静滞魔能方尖塔` panel states:
- **Grade `传说`**, object type **territory building**, **does not require construction after acquisition** and can be placed within owned territory.
- Printed building area `350平方` (the panel does **not explicitly append square-metre units**); **height 32 m**.
- **Magical energy reservoir 0/1,000,000**, as observed at inspection. Neither an initial refill nor initial shield deployment is shown.
- On successful placement, covers the **whole territory** with a translucent blue defensive shield; explicitly resists **physical, magical, and mental** attacks.
- Shield HP **1,000,000,000 / 1,000,000,000** in its native panel. **After taking damage**, the panel states **10,000 mana energy per second** is spent to restore **1,000,000 shield HP per second**; not a free perpetual regeneration while energy remains 0.
- Upgrades **may** increase shield HP but no level recipe, upgrade quantity or formula is printed. After shield breaks, **1-hour self-recovery**, during which recovery speed **+300%**. Do not fabricate the precise post-break HP or energy handling.
- **Original-protagonist inventory possession only**; Cao Xing discusses intending to deploy it in his future huge city, not a completed deployment in this inspected scene.

## FX31-0006 — Attack stasis domain on the building

Panel effect `攻击静滞领域` automatically triggers **0.5 seconds before hostile attack contact with the shield**:
- projectile speed -> **1/100**;
- mental-attack transmission speed -> **1/100**;
- melee attacker suffers **20% slow**, and after **three stacks**, **2-second freeze**.

Those are exact panel effects, **not** a total time stop, invulnerability, instant kill, universal field radius or arbitrary slow of other players outside this building/territory context.

## FX31-0008 — Arcard's text-only World Anchor design and token

Arcard's dated original-world **letter**, actually received (`SX-0401`), states that ancient Asura labs placed spatial nodes at the landing sites of future otherworldly Lords. Using the received **`世界之锚钥匙`** (`SX-0402`) to activate an existing **`世界之锚`** apparatus is intended to anchor those territories to this **specific ruin**, enabling **territory -> ruin transit**. The letter's warning about Asura's fog disaster, demigod-scale `三相湮灭炮` failure, and future global balance is **original narrative lore**, not a free disaster event on the new LL01 calendar. The object `世界之锚` was a **pre-existing fixed installation**, not an inventory item, new magic structure blueprint, nor evidence that the obelisk can make portals.

## FX31-0009 — Quantitative anchor limits in Ch731

Arcard's letter says it can activate **up to 100,000 spatial anchor points in one day**, with **seven days' wait before reuse**. Cao Xing's dialogue/interior monologue interprets this as **up to 100,000 otherworldly Lords in a run**, and later considers giving slots to his preferred four regions. Preserve the evidence tiers: literal **spatial anchor points per day** in letter; **people/entry slot equivalence is his interpretation**, not an independently metered transported-passenger count in the inspected passage. Eligibility selection is his *plan*, not proof of an arbitrary cross-server permission API or successful activation in these paragraphs. The chapter recalls an earlier **20,000 reputation** award on entering the treasure room; it is **already received earlier** and does **not** establish a second reward in Ch731. The later mass teleport operation requires follow-up proof, not an automatic T0 unlock.
