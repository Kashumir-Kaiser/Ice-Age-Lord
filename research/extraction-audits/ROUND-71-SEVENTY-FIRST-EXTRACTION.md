# Round 71 — Seventy-First Extraction (Chapters 1122–1131)

Sequential source review, research-only. Nothing in this ledger becomes LL01 starting inventory, guaranteed new-game loot, or a change to the fixed event calendar.

## Sources and method

- Full chapter text was read on [Bqquge](https://www.bqquge.org/851), following each visible continuation through Chapters 1122–1131. Titles and order were cross-checked in the separate [Bq8xsw chapter directory](https://www.bq8xsw.com/chapterlist/110975.html).
- Vietnamese checks now include readable catalog/index surfaces across [Cơm Nguội](https://comnguoinovel.com/truyen/tan-the-bang-phong-ta-xay-dung-hoan-my-lanh-dia), [Xa Lộ Sách](https://xalosach.com/bang-phong-tan-the-ta-che-tao-hoan-my-lanh-dia-0.html), [Bách Ngọc Sách](https://bachngocsach.me/truyen/doc-quyen-dich-bang-phong-tan-the-ta-che-tao-hoan-my-lanh-dia), its separate [audio catalog](https://bachngocsach.me/truyen/audio-dich-bang-phong-tan-the-ta-che-tao-hoan-my-lanh-dia), and the [NovOne catalog mirror](https://novone.site/works/bang-phong-tan-the-ta-che-tao-hoan-my-lanh-dia).
- Cơm Nguội Ch1127's displayed title matches Chinese printed Ch1124, confirming +3 numbering at this arc. Cơm Nguội Ch1116 likewise matches Chinese printed Ch1113. Both chapter bodies show a login gate, so Vietnamese titles are used only for alignment, not reward quantities.
- Xa Lộ Sách directory page 12 lists Vietnamese Ch1124 as “Cùng Ma Chủ đàm phán, điều kiện của Tào Tinh,” a different story point from Chinese printed Ch1124's World-Eye blueprint. Do not align Xa Lộ Sách to Chinese chapter numbers by raw number. Bách Ngọc Sách editions also use segmented numbering (4,253 and 2,774 segments); NovOne mirrors 2,734 Xa Lộ Sách links.
- Round 71 adds 32 rows, SX-1141–SX-1172: final-phase rules, campaign settlement grants, one sold-out market order and proceeds, pooled spoils, Dragon Soul Envoy trial rules, a full hidden-shop purchase, and two observed chest outcomes.

## Deduplication and limits

- Chapter 1124's 762-yi wallet display is a cumulative total after the 70-yi-equivalent meat sale. The ledger records only the 70,000,000,000 sale proceeds.
- Chapter 1125's 767.1-yi total and 2,748-crystal / 3,085-rune-stone inventory values are cumulative display context; named battle-spoil receipts are separate.
- Chapter 1126's complete shop cost is one transaction record; individual purchased-item records do not repeat the input cost.
- The two chest contents in Chapter 1127 are observed opening outcomes only. No chest contents are extrapolated.
- The Round 70 Vietnamese +4 chapter offset claim was a title-match mistake; correction v7.0.1 replaces it with the two title-confirmed +3 anchors. No Round 70 Chinese-derived item facts or quantities change.
