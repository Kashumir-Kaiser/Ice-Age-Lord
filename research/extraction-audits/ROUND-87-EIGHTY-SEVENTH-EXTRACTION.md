## Chapter findings

- **1282:** Cao Xing inventories the pooled Cleaner rewards. The list explicitly includes the Demon-Slayer Medal, Demon-Slaying Throat Ballista blueprint, Dawn Sacred-Bone Beacon, and free God-grade building blueprint in addition to rewards already recorded in Ch1279–1280 and existing Godheads/scrolls. He opens the Free Forbidden Weapon Chest and selects the high-level Harvest Scythe. No duplicate rows were added for the already-logged Free Forbidden Weapon Chest, Shears, Godheads, Ancient Red Sky Chests, or Myriad Shields True Solution.
- **1283:** Item panels detail the purified Apocalypse Godhead, Demon-Slayer Medal, special crossbow blueprint, and Dawn Beacon. The medal is level 230, Lower God Artifact, with 40/100 Apocalypse Demon-origin progress. This resolves Round 86's missing-medal note; the source does not split the medal's progress between the two Cleaner kills.
- **1284:** Six World Protector Marks are awarded. Cao Xing opens the free God-grade land-building blueprint chest and selects the Eternal Dragon Spine: Divine Domain Barrier Wall blueprint. He opens the two Ancient Red Sky Chests previously earned and gets Ten-Thousand-Year Tree Hearts ×2 (Semi-God) and a Black Crystal Staff ×1 (Semi-Artifact).
- **1285:** Botier transfers pooled battle spoils to Cao Xing. Recorded explicit names and quantities: Wood Crystals ×1,231; Mordema Demon-Vine Magnets ×62,151; Random Magic Seed Bags ×342,500; Master-grade Elemental Crystals ×15,640,000; Corrupted Redemption Godhead (Upper God) ×1; Fallen Angel Wings ×1 pair; Circular Dark Origin Essence ×3,202; Wraith Crystals ×4,510,000; Netherworld Black Steel ×1,420,000; and a distinct Master-grade Elemental Crystals ×12,190,000 receipt. The Medium/Large Attribute Books line and trailing loot list are truncated; quantity and omitted items remain unknown.
- **1286:** Fourth-stage territory expansion and floating-continent plans are discussed; nothing is recorded as built or received.
- **1287:** Flame Dragon Tooth and Primordial Scale crafting is discussed, but no finished dagger or other weapon is acquired in the chapter.
- **1288:** Botier fuses one Phaimordus Purest Blood Essence from existing stock, completing his fifth bloodline. Cao Xing hands the previously acquired Observer's Eye to Olive Ás; the fusion is still pending at the visible chapter end.
- **1289:** The Truth Eye/Observer's Eye fusion is completed as the Truth-Observer Eye. Hilda fuses her existing Black-Blood Godhead and becomes Scarlet Mother Goddess. The system announces day 190 of the second development phase and the approaching final expansion pack; this does not change the fixed event calendar.
- **1290:** Ancient Sword Magdel and Su Haxin fuse into Ancient Sword — Sovereign Authority (level 200), then bind to Liu Muxue. The same chapter calls it a Quasi-Artifact at fusion and a Semi-Artifact at binding; quality is left unresolved. Liu Muxue merges Angel Wings and the newly received Fallen Angel Wings, enabling Twelve-Wing Archangel and Fallen Angel forms.
- **1291:** Bingbing fuses the Catastrophe Godhead SX-1460 and advances to Upper God, controlling the Catastrophe rule. This is recorded as use of an existing item, not a second receipt.
