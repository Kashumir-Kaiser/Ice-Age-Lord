## 3. Source mechanics and new source structures

### War Research Institute — chapter 520
- **Original source blueprint** drops in aggregate from six Gold Chests.
- New building *Viện Nghiên Cứu Chiến Tranh*: can research Arrow Tower Strengthening (+10% attack/range each level; 5 levels) and Wall Reinforcement (+10% defense/durability each level; 5 levels). These are purchased research operations, not free passive bonuses.
- Size **675 m²**, height **14 m**. Original novel requires **Energy Tower level 11**, **100,000 stone**, **50,000 iron**, **10,000 copper**, **5,000 mithril**, **1 Master Elemental Crystal**, **231 construction hours** and max **4 workers**. **Never** map Energy Tower 11 to Lí Lan Survival Fire 11 by name or assumption.
- Separate related blueprint **Imperial Knight Training Camp** requires real training books to train eligible civilians; **Expert Lighthouse Upgrade** modifies an existing Advanced Lighthouse, rather than creating a new building.

### Distinct chest batches — chapter 520
- Six Gold Chests, three Moon Chests and three Ancient Moon Chests each produce a **batch receipt**. Preserve original observed quantities; no exact drop weights or per-chest promises follow from these examples.
- Chapter 886 special case: personal S-quest 90% and 100% stage rewards (Holy Spear, 50 Divine Fragments, Light Godhead, selectable legendary relic box); Light Godfire is a **separate defeated-boss drop**, not a quest stage reward.
- Chapter 424 Bách Niên Thụ Tâm from the 100,000-point Epic Wheel grows an eligible magical plant by 100 years; the existing Neisha legendary ring from the 1,000,000-point wheel remains **in the parent registry** and is not duplicated.
- Tyrant Skull: Epic helmet; listed Strength+800, VIT+500, crit+30%, protection against head hits and a triggered attack-speed/crit-damage effect. Source details are extractable as a separate item contract.
- Stone Flux Pauldron: base Strength +100%, attack speed -50% (actual trade-off).
- Laser Scope: head slot replaces helmet; +100% extra critical damage on ranged physical critical hits.
- Loyalty Pearl: sets a member to maximum loyalty or death-loyalty; redundant for Lí Lan directly revived undead, useful for eligible living units if adopted.

### First-clear, ordinary clear, and leaderboard must be separate — chapter 519
- The **Death Walker** title belongs to the **first global** tenth-wave clear, and chapter 520 provides its effects: Spirit +5%, all skill cooldowns -10%, immune to corpse poison and plague.
- Wave ten completion grants 1 Ancient Moon Chest, 3 Divine-Power Fragments and 500 Golden Points.
- Rank one settlement adds 1 Moon Chest and 100 Golden Points.
- A later total of 3 Bronze + 3 Silver + 6 Gold + 3 Moon + 3 Ancient Moon Chests describes what the protagonist had **after** those payouts. It is not another grant.

### Lucky God Wheel — chapter 932, EV20
- Twelve segments. 3, 6, 9 and 12 signal **demigod-tier challenges**.
- Player chooses **one existing friendly challenger**; the source excludes anyone above Legendary.
- A successful challenge pays its segment reward. Up to **two further rolls** are possible if the reward is declined after success.
- Failure has a source-stated chance of challenger death; numerical death probability and each segment’s exact loot are **not established here**.
- This is a site challenge, **not a generic event-item shop**. For deterministic implementation, preseed roll outcomes in the world save and preserve accepted consequences.

## 4. Critical deduplication and exclusion findings
- EV18 personal quest milestones are not granted to every lord who joins a world event, nor does every defeated boss automatically yield the original protagonist’s exact rolled loot.
- EV08/EV09 new shop purchase rows represent **observed transaction quantity**, not global purchase caps. Do not repopulate shop using those quantities as max stock.
- Existing pass 2 already contains `EV10` wave-clear chest rewards (ch480, 481, 483). The new ch519/520 receipts belong to **EV11**, a later separate siege challenge during the mist-disaster episode.
- In ch520 the chest stock counts overlap directly paid rank and wave-clear ch519 items; do not double-count.
- The 3 Ancient Moon chests were opened and yielded five observed lines, but nothing supports five lines **per chest**.
- Ch484 gives cumulative 2 Moon and 6 Gold chests after EV10 settlement, but does not prove these were additive rewards beyond what earlier wave and rank receipts already granted. Its recurring weekly event wording is **a direct source-vs-LL01 fixed schedule conflict**.
- Chapters 1079–1137 contain highly relevant later event-shop chapter titles. The full inventories/prices were not verified and appear **only as research leads**, not 20 invented shop rows.
- Ch790 `开罐之王` hidden achievement was observed from a world jar-opening sequence before the Dream event, and is *not* assigned to EV14.
