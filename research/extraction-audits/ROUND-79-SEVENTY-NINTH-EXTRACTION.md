## Chapter findings

- **1202 — 五枚神格，审讯古神:** Title confirms a five-Godhead haul. The official preview opens on arena rankings and does not expose the item panel.
- **1203 — 再收三神，特殊的增益技能:** Title confirms three additional gods and a special buff. The readable opening shows Cao Xing interrogating the captured Vine Ancient God, but the source preview does not identify all three gods or expose the buff.
- **1204 — 解封，诺恩圣女苏醒！:** Zola/Norn Saint, previously believed dead, was frozen in the deep-sea ice abyss and recovered into Cao Xing’s territory. The chapter is about her awakening.
- **1205 — 收服诺恩圣女，领地第一尊上位神！:** Zola says she can no longer help in battle. Cao Xing says her divine body and Godhead are badly damaged, including rule-level cracks, but claims restoration may be possible. No restorative item or completed repair is shown.
- **1206 — 卓拉真神？重返巅峰的方法！:** The Pale Sacred Tree’s source-tracing ability is available once after recovery; Zola’s restoration is discussed, without a named item receipt in the visible excerpt.
- **1207 — 第五波全体赐福，数十万自爆大军！:** Cao Xing’s backpack contains five new Godheads; three have potential fusion candidates. Each new god needs at least 30,000 Divine Power. One Ice Dragon Crystal restores 50 Divine Power, equal to five Divine Power Fragments. A similar Wood Crystal effect is only hypothesized. See SX-1386–1387.
- **1208 — 偿还恩情，远征南大陆！:** Cao Xing leads an expedition south and sends nearly 100,000 troops through the cross-region array. This is an event, not an item receipt.
- **1209 — 天降女神，让圣焰净化一切！:** The expedition reaches an enormous ominous forest; a powerful ancient evil presence is sensed.
- **1210 — 雷神祸乱，曹星的猎物！:** Thor’s attack breaks the elf barrier, destroys many trees, and causes many elf casualties. No reward is visible.
- **1211 — 风华绝代，南大陆第一女王。:** The South Continent crisis continues; the accessible opening shows no protagonist-side item receipt.
