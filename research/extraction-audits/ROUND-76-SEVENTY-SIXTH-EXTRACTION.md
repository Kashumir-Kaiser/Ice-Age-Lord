## Chapter findings

- **1172:** Ailin’s seven-thorn soul judgment ends with Cao Xing catching the final thorn. She begins resisting the Seat’s control; no item or reward.
- **1173:** Ailin severs the soul brand and joins as midgod Iv260. The system shows population +200, prosperity +100,000, human fortune +100,000, loyalty 100, and a Gods’ Lord title buff (+1 Divine Power regeneration for all members, −1% Divine skill cooldown). Combat effects remain context.
- **1174:** Ironwall Sage Alphonse refuses surrender and is killed. His visible drops are Guardian Godhead (midgod) ×1, Holy Shield · Wall of the Word ×1, and Mace · Child’s Holocaust ×1; the visible XP is 3316.26 yi for Cao Xing and 1658.13 yi team XP. The drop line is truncated, so no other drops are asserted.
- **1175:** The existing Godslayer sword gains +100,000 attack per divine kill and has a conditional Quasi-artifact evolution at 100 kills. This weapon counter is not assumed to match the separate SS mission counter.
- **1176:** Nektar’s explicit reward is Bone Garden God blood ×1, Divine Essence Crystals ×10, and Prestige +50,000 (halved for a kill outside the main plane). Subsequent Urgos/Aovix panels have garbled/duplicated labels and are left unattributed. Inventory aggregate: 39 Spirit Flames and 12 Mutated Overlord Souls; some scroll lines/names are omitted.
- **1177:** Abyss Terminator reaches 100/100. Malenina’s divine-power spend and combat abilities are recorded only as mechanics/context. The awakened Nether King forces evacuation; no mission settlement is visible yet.
- **1178:** Rule seal breaks fully and gods awaken worldwide. No direct inventory receipt.
- **1179:** Global system announcement opens the Age of Gods Descending and Faction System. Divine Chosen receive a god’s blessings/power but become bound to that deity’s faction, must fight for it, and are subject to a measure of divine control.
- **1180:** Cao Xing plans to create more divine allies from three previously prepared Godheads and holds the Light Godhead for Isabel. This is planning/previous inventory, not a new receipt. Oliviaus explains Godhead fusion preparation.
- **1181:** The SS mission settles. Its visible stage rewards include a Random Legendary Equipment Chest ×1, Choice Relic Chests ×3, six named semi-artifact armor pieces, Crimson Sky Chest ×1, Flawless Divine Fire ×1, one choice Godhead (Cao Xing selects Crystal), Random Divine Skill Chest ×1, title Gods Terminator, and Prestige +1,000,000. The end shows three chests being opened, but not their contents. The stage lines total 3,200 Divine Power Fragments while later prose says 3,400; both figures are preserved as a conflict. Godhead refinement is described as requiring at least 80% divine-nature purity and 30,000–100,000 Divine Power per person; Cao Xing calculates 150,000 for his and three allies’ planned refinement. This is a future plan, not a completed spend or breakthrough.
