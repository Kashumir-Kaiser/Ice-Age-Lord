## Newly appended source effects

### FX22-0001 — Blood Demon Hatchery / `SX-0080`
Epic; footprint **472 m²**, armor **5,000**, durability **100,000**. Produces **2 sixth-tier Blood Demons per day** with supply consumption **500 liters of blood per hour**. Blood species and strength are unrestricted for eligibility; stronger source blood increases Elite/Headman chances with no published probability. Build cost: blood crystal 1,000; wood 50,000; stone 50,000; refined iron 10,000; mithril 1,000. Production is not free and requires actual blood logistics.

### FX22-0002 — Blood Altar / `SX-0081`
Epic; footprint **145 m²**, armor **10,000**, durability **100,000**. Construction: blood crystal 500, wood 20,000, stone 30,000, refined iron 5,000, rune stone 500. Sacrificed corpses yield same-level Vampire creatures by corpse quality family: normal → inferior; elite/headman → lower; leader/commander → middle; dominator → high; chances are **not quantified**, with upper groups expressly small. No guaranteed resurrection, XP preservation, or link to Lí Lan's SSS revival.

### FX22-0003 — Blood Cannon crafting blueprint
Epic, purchased and learned in Chapter 638 for **10 million war merits** as one of the four blueprints. Craft in Blacksmith with **Master Forging**. Attack **89,452–91,432**, range **4,500 m**, **30-m** full-damage splash and **1–3 random blood curses**. Materials: blood crystal 20; refined iron 20,000; mithril 1,000; master elemental crystal 200. No ammunition loading is required. **Effect-only record**, because this round does not add an unverified item identity to existing SX list. There is no unlimited vendor stock guarantee.

### FX22-0004 — Legendary Blood Destroyer Tower / `SX-0083`
Chapter 762 reveals **two built operational towers**. Each is **58 m** tall, armor **100,000**, durability **5,000,000**, no native attack stat. It summons a Blood Destroyer that hits all enemies in a **5,000-m target area** for **16,891,642–17,315,623** and remains for **10 seconds** to attack locally. **60-second** firing interval; maximum firing distance **100 km**. These distances are independent and must not be conflated.
