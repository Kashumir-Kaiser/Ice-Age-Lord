## Chapter findings

- **1232 — 必杀目标，融入规则。:** The accessible QQ opening discusses the Cleaners’ kill target and integration of remaining Godheads into rules; it shows no protagonist-side item receipt (SX-1400).
- **1233 — 新·湖女之剑与神国扩张:** The Lake Goddess provides a new Lake Goddess Sword for Assam. The text says it raises her battle strength by at least 20% (SX-1401).
- **1234 — 完整的冰雪法典，绑定神器法袍。:** The previously held Ice and Snow Codex completes repair after roughly three to four months of work and returns as a complete mid-tier artifact. This is a state change, not a new acquisition (SX-1402).
- **1235 — 神器的隐藏效果，冰龙王族战躯！:** Cao Xing binds the already-owned Snow Burial Star Robe; the Lucky Seeker title adds 400 luck. It can expand to tens of kilometres. Its Celestial Curtain Shield inherits 100% of the owner’s HP, armor and damage reduction and ends when owner HP drops below 30% (SX-1403–SX-1404).
- **1236 — 第一赛季结束，清点奖励。:** 14,600 Random Ancient Coin Bags are opened. Outputs: 50,253 Bronze, 6,195 Silver, 1,142 Gold, 519 Moonlight and 215 Redcloud Chixiao coins. Exchanges yield 503 Bronze, 62 Silver, 12 Gold, 5 Moonlight and 2 Redcloud Chixiao chests. Named observed results include Century Tree Hearts ×3, Earth Crystal, Enchanted Broken Blade, Water Essence, Dark Silver Guard Hammer, Bloodcry Dagger, Hero Hearts ×12 and Master Strength Hearts ×341. Exact chest-tier attribution for each named result is not isolated, and random results are not guaranteed (SX-1405–SX-1415).
- **1237 — 赛事主办权，神级斗兽场。:** First-season prizes include 70,000 Dragon Coins, a Divine-grade Ancient Beast Arena model, one self-select Legendary Equipment chest, one self-select Relic chest, one self-select Demigod Skill chest, one Divine Fire and one Ancient Redcloud Chest. Cao Xing receives hosting rights for the first Dragon Cup. The arena has adjustable internal scale, 191 environments, contestant equalization and death-prevention teleport (SX-1416–SX-1421).
- **1238 — 幽影套装集齐，霜之哀伤:** The season Legendary chest yields Shadow Wings for Sara, completing her Shadow set; the Relic chest yields Water Essence for Aili; the Demigod Skill chest yields Frostmourne: Blade of Ending for Cao Xing. He exchanges 70,000 Dragon Coins for 7,000 Random Coin Bags, producing 23,491 Bronze, 3,182 Silver, 488 Gold, 316 Moonlight and 104 Redcloud Chixiao coins. Three Moonlight chests yield a War Saint’s Broken Blade relic for Talia, a separate receipt from the earlier Ch755 copy (SX-1422–SX-1426).
- **1239 — 自选半神技，泰坦能源之心。:** The chapter title identifies the Titan Energy Heart. The next chapter’s opening describes a metallic energy sphere and Tong Chuchu’s interest, but exact stats and a transfer are not visible; no receipt to Tong Chuchu is asserted (SX-1427).
- **1240 — 对抗清理者的配方，黄金瞳！:** The title names a formula against the Cleaners and Golden Eye. Accessible preview text is occupied by discussion of the energy heart; ingredients, Golden Eye mechanics, and any receipt remain unverified (SX-1428).
- **1241 — 兽神之心，冰冰进化:** The opening describes the Beast God Heart as a bloodline treasure capable of unlocking ultimate evolution. Exact grade/mechanics and acquisition details are unavailable (SX-1429).
