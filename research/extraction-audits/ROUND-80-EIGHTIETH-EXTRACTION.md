# Round 80 — Eightieth Extraction (Chapters 1212–1221)

Sequentially reviewed printed Chinese Chapters 1212–1221. This checkpoint adds eight source records (SX-1388–SX-1395), bringing the cumulative ledger to 1395 data rows across 69 columns. All entries remain research candidates and do not activate new-game inventory.

## Chapter findings

- **1212 — 单杀雷神，神格到手！:** Cao Xing survives Thor’s strongest attack and kills him. The title explicitly confirms a Thunder Godhead is obtained. The accessible body opening does not show rank, drop panel, or any other loot; only one named Godhead is recorded (SX-1388).
- **1213 — 雷神法典，为圣女补魔。:** The Thunder God Codex is named and used to replenish a Saint. Acquisition scene and replenishment amount are not visible; its existence/use is recorded without asserting a new receipt (SX-1389).
- **1214 — 铁匠突破，半神级锻造师:** The expedition continues; a blacksmith breaks through to demigod-level forging. No item receipt confirmed.
- **1215 — 集齐法典残卷，两处战场的惊人收益:** Five Legendary Proficiency Emblems are explicitly confirmed as loot, with enough potential to promote Francisca to demigod alchemist (SX-1390).
- **1216 — 百万魔法种子，合成神格的途径:** 1.62 million Random Magic Seed Bags are stated. Each yields 1–10 seeds; seed quality ranges from green to legendary, with a very small chance of demigod seeds (SX-1391).
- **1217 — 神级秘药配方:** A God-level potion formula enters the story. The adjacent readable chapter excerpt shows the handover to Francisca in Ch1218; no ingredients or potion are counted as acquired.
- **1218 — 深夜拜访，女王的请求:** Cao Xing gives Francisca an unnamed God-level potion formula and says he will gather 100 ingredient shares. The formula transfer is recorded; the ingredients remain a future promise (SX-1392).
- **1219 — 伯捷之父，血脉融合:** Bojie’s four bloodlines combine in his fight against his father. No item receipt confirmed.
- **1220 — 混沌之神降临，两个月:** Chaos gods arrive/discuss an impending threat. No protagonist-side item receipt confirmed.
- **1221 — 第三阶段扩建完工，兵强马壮:** New gods Andrew, Francisca, and Zevelin ascend. Legendary Resurrected Army kills in Myguma Forest can drop Wood Crystals. Gathering every 25 days produces crystal stores worth 30,000 Divine Power; about 80,000 Divine Power is accumulated over two months, while natural regeneration is separately converted to fragments. 9,000 Divine Power Fragments are consumed for the three ascensions, leaving about 10,000 Divine Power. Andrew’s named skill 狂野之形·原始解放 is visible, but its detailed effect is truncated (SX-1393–1395).
