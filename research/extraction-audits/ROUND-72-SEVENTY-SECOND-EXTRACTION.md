Sequential source review; research-only. Nothing extracted here becomes LL01 starting inventory, guaranteed new-game loot, or a change to the fixed event calendar.

## Sources and method

- Read Bqquge's Chinese text through its visible continuation pages for each printed chapter, and cross-checked title/order in the separate Bq8xsw chapter directory.
- Checked multiple Vietnamese editions/catalogs: [Cơm Nguội](https://comnguoinovel.com/truyen/tan-the-bang-phong-ta-xay-dung-hoan-my-lanh-dia), [Hắc Hoàng Đại Đế](https://hachoangdaide.online/tan-the-bang-phong-ta-xay-dung-hoan-my-lanh-dia), [Xa Lộ Sách](https://xalosach.com/bang-phong-tan-the-ta-che-tao-hoan-my-lanh-dia-0.html), and the [Bách Ngọc Sách](https://bachngocsach.me/truyen/doc-quyen-dich-bang-phong-tan-the-ta-che-tao-hoan-my-lanh-dia) catalog plus Vietnamese index mirrors.
- Vietnamese numbering is not interchangeable with Chinese print numbering. Cơm Nguội and Hắc Hoàng Ch1133 both display “Jormag thức tỉnh, ác ý từ Tử Vong Cự Long!”, whereas Chinese printed Ch1133 is the ranking settlement. Xa Lộ Sách's Ch1124 also names a different story point from Chinese print Ch1124. Use matched titles/story anchors; never align by raw chapter number alone.
- The Cơm Nguội and Hắc Hoàng chapter bodies are login-gated; Xa Lộ Sách offers a short preview and gates the rest behind its app. Vietnamese sources were used for visible title/catalog alignment only. No login or app restriction was bypassed, and no reward quantity below relies on gated text.
- Round 72 adds 67 rows, SX-1173–SX-1239, with 69 CSV columns. The cumulative CSV now has 1,239 data rows. One source_chapter typo on the four promotions row was corrected to printed Ch1141 before checkpointing.

## Contradictions and limits

- The shop's weapon name is 极冰之刃; the receipt and most later text use 极光之刃, and one later line uses 千冰之刃. The ledger preserves the naming conflict as one purchase, not three.
- Wallet arithmetic: 67,512 displayed − 67,510 listed cost = 2; the receipt independently claims 67,150 spent. Keep both claims with no invented reconciliation.
- Five Dragon Hearts are bought. The Ch1138 plan names five destinations; Ch1140 confirms four gifts and does not show the incubating egg receiving one. The fifth delivery remains unconfirmed.
- Legendary Hearts are used for breakthroughs, but their acquisition is not shown in these chapters.
- Arena rewards reported in chat remain low-confidence claims, not a recovered official tier table.
- A Red Sky Chest result from Ch1134 remains a single observed opening, never a guaranteed drop contract.
