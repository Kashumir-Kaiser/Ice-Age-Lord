# Round 70 — Seventieth Extraction (Chapters 1112–1121)

Sequential source review, research-only. Nothing in this ledger becomes LL01 starting inventory, guaranteed new-game loot, or a change to the fixed event calendar.

## Sources and method

- Primary chapter text: [Bqquge Chapter 1112–1121 directory](https://www.bqquge.org/851), with chapter text read through the linked continuation pages. Printed titles and order were checked against the separate [Bq8xsw directory](https://www.bq8xsw.com/chapterlist/110975.html).
- Phase II's repeatable rates were read in [Bq8xsw Chapter 1111](https://www.bq8xsw.com/10082436/11075759.html), then applied only to explicit kill/clear totals later in this batch.
- Vietnamese sources searched across multiple editions: [Cơm Nguội Novel](https://comnguoinovel.com/truyen/tan-the-bang-phong-ta-xay-dung-hoan-my-lanh-dia), [Bách Ngọc Sách](https://bachngocsach.me/truyen/doc-quyen-dich-bang-phong-tan-the-ta-che-tao-hoan-my-lanh-dia), [Xa Lộ Sách](https://xalosach.com/bang-phong-tan-the-ta-che-tao-hoan-my-lanh-dia-0.html), [NovOne catalog mirror](https://novone.site/works/bang-phong-tan-the-ta-che-tao-hoan-my-lanh-dia), TruyệnYY, and Truyện Trung. Cơm Nguội's Vietnamese Ch.1116 title matches Chinese printed Ch.1112, establishing a +4 numbering offset there; its body asks readers to log in. Bách Ngọc Sách and TruyệnYY split the novel into 4,253 segments; Xa Lộ Sách lists 2,734 chapters, also cataloged by NovOne. The latter sources were used to compare editions and numbering, not to assert current quantities.
- The ledger adds 19 rows, SX-1122–SX-1140: quantified nest-clear rewards, divine-kill rewards and derived increments, plus two blessing effects.

## Deduplication and derived quantities

- Nest rewards: Ch1113's 115 is cumulative and includes the Ch1112 receipt. The dataset logs 1 clear in Ch1112 and 114 in Ch1113, preserving a combined 115.
- Divine kills: cumulative progression is 5 in Ch1115, 3 more in Ch1116, and 495 after six days in Ch1119. Only 487 are new in the last snapshot. Scores, crystals, and Gold Points for the 3 and 487 kills are calculated from the Ch1111 per-kill rule and labeled as derived, not presented as individually enumerated receipt text.

## Source disagreements and limits

- Vietnamese edition chapter numbers do not align uniformly. Cơm Nguội's displayed Ch1116 is the same translated title as Chinese print Ch1112. Bách Ngọc Sách and TruyệnYY use split segments; Xa Lộ Sách / NovOne catalog 2,734 chapter links. Every Vietnamese match therefore requires a title check; raw chapter counts are not a mapping.
- No Vietnamese full-text page for this exact arc was both accessible and aligned to printed numbering. Cơm Nguội requests login; no login was attempted. Factual quantities are cross-checked on two Chinese text sites, not inferred from Vietnamese catalog metadata.
- Ch1112's second nest reward text is truncated and omitted.
- The Chapter 1116 reward quantities and the Chapter 1119 residual are computed by applying the source's already stated per-kill reward. The explicit kill counts are preserved as the basis.
- The two Chapter 1118 blessings are effects and do not create consumable entries.
- Campaign settlement follows in later chapters and is outside this batch.
