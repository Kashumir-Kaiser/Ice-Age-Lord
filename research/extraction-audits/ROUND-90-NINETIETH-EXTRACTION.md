## Chapter findings

- **1312:** Sarah recovers one Upper God Original Godhead, 3,124 Divine Power Fragments, a Natural Rule Bracelet (Half-God Artifact), and an Unknown Treasure Map with coordinates 2145,1623,331,623 from the Original inheritance site. She also removes an old wooden chest from the tree, but the text does not explicitly say it is opened or map the listed rewards to its contents. The caretaker separately grants an Original Rule inheritance sphere for Andrew, containing lifetime insights and two unnamed divine skills. Ancient War Site material totals shown here are cumulative snapshots, not new batches.
- **1313:** Bron's Body Tower challenge continues; Skadi offers an Ancient Hunt God inheritance-site coordinate in exchange for tower access details. Cao Xing does not transfer those details or a reward. No item receipt.
- **1314:** Twelve additional ancient Divine corpses are converted in the western region; the Tomb of Gods total reaches 60. No discrete material batch is separately totaled.
- **1315:** Bron's first-floor Giant Race clear awards a Great Essence Potion, which permanently adds 30% Constitution and is consumed. Three western inheritance sites yield three unnamed inheritance spheres, three Upper Godheads identified as Light, Sky Hunt, and Dark, and 12,416 Divine Power Fragments. The spheres are assigned to Isabel (Light), Isabel's second personality (Dark), and Renes (Sky Hunt). Bron is supplied 2.2 million Ancient Soulstone Fragments and synthesizes 22 Ancient Soulstones. Sarah is supplied 1.6 million fragments and later synthesizes 16 Ancient Soulstones. The chapter's eight-day stock totals (including 823,000,000 Corroded Ancient Weapon Fragments; 25,420,000 Faith Crystals; 11,320,000 Ancient Soulstone Fragments; and 34,970,000 Dim Flame Crystals) are snapshots, not new receipts.
- **1316:** Cao Xing transfers Sarah one existing World Guardian Mark, the missing Agility Tower entry item. Sarah's trial begins; no tower reward is claimed yet.
- **1317:** Bron clears Body Tower floor two, Heavenly Army Gate, receiving the Half-God Artifact Heavenly Army World Bracer and a permanent +20% base armor bonus. Cao Xing supplies Holly, Sufu, and Lẫm Đông with matching Godheads and distributes 9,000 Divine Power Fragments in three 3,000-fragment shares for their breakthroughs. Their ascension notices identify the Godheads as Upper God Assassination, Middle God Wan-Yu Divine Lord, and Middle God Giant Wolf, respectively. Their prior inventory source is not identified in this range.
- **1318:** Sarah's first Agility Tower trial awards a Wind Gale Essence potion, which she consumes. Her second-floor clear awards permanent +35% Critical Damage and the Half-God Artifact Void Mask, which she equips. The Human Emperor inheritance for Mạnh Nghị begins but remains in progress.
- **1319:** Bron clears Body Tower floor three. The unique reward adds 200% Constitution to future level gains and retroactively recalculates prior gains. The translation reports about 320 million Constitution after correction; one intermediate numeral is malformed. Bron proceeds to the fourth-floor trial.
- **1320:** The team enters the eastern Ancient War Site. Three inheritance locations are found: Sword God, emotion-path, and Holy Flame. The emotion inheritance awards one Upper God Seven Emotions and Six Desires Godhead, an unstated “several thousand” Divine Power Fragments, and one unnamed inheritance sphere. Cao Xing assigns an existing Upper God Spirit Energy Godhead to Olive Ás. The resource totals after four days are cumulative snapshots and are not counted again.
- **1321:** Bron clears Body Tower floor four and receives one growth-type God Artifact embryo and one Upper God Frost Chill Godhead. He fuses both into his existing Beginning Shield, evolving it into an Upper God Artifact; no distinct final name is stated. Bron begins floor five, which is not completed in this range. Sarah and Botier are still in floor-four challenges.
