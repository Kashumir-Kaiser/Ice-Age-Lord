## Chapter findings

- **1262 — 沉眠之渊平定，斩杀堕天使！** After a seven-day battle, the twelve-winged fallen angel is destroyed, leaving an intact pair of black wings and a dark godhead. Botier collects both; neither is shown entering Cao Xing’s inventory. Tong Chuchu demonstrates her self-created lower-god skill, First Sequence: Titan Stomp (5,000 divine power; 5,000 km radius; two-second immobilization; stated damage formula).
- **1263 — 女武神 VS 女武神，两位妖孽的对决！** Marlene Nina continues the ancient Valkyrie duel. No inheritance receipt or item award is completed.
- **1264 — 超越前身，属于那一位的骄傲！** Marlene Nina wins. The ancient Valkyrie begins transferring her full inheritance, Godhead, and Nirvana rule, but an interruption occurs before completion.
- **1265 — 战争之神现世，女武神的执着！** Balthazar explains the interruption and announces four gift groups: a True Flame Godhead, dragon egg, three Phaimordus materials, and Burning Heaven Demon Crystal. The item receipt lines appear in Ch1266, so this chapter is not counted as a second receipt.
- **1266 — 四件至宝，真·火焰神格！** Six system receipts are explicit: True Flame Godhead ×1; Unknown Egg ×1; Phaimordus Purest Blood Essence ×114; Primordial Scale ×1; Flame Dragon Tooth ×1; Burning Heaven Demon Crystal ×1. Item functions are recorded as claims/descriptions; no fusion, hatching, crafting, or use is shown.
- **1267 — 火焰龙牙，涅槃重生的女武神！** Material tooltips detail the dragon tooth/scale crafting uses and bloodline-fusion conditions. Marlene Nina completes her inheritance, with system-reported +1,000% base stats, divine-limit combat proficiencies, permanent Nirvana-fire territory effects, and level cap 300. Zola reconstructs her divine body and regains her Godhead, domain and skills; Nirvana Shelter grants immunity to continuous damage. These are progression outcomes, not item receipts.
- **1268 — 圣女恢复，踏入真神之境！** Zola activates the Destroyer set form, with +300% all stats and temporary True God-level power. Eternal Power makes the form persistent at 10 divine power per second and lengthens the ultimate cooldown to five natural days. Cao Xing explicitly hands Zola the previously acquired magic spear 泣血箜鸣 (existing identity SX-0938); the transfer is recorded as SX-1447. No new spear grade or evolution occurs.
- **1269 — 圣座阴谋，纵横混沌** A cutaway reveals the Seed Sower’s plans involving the Holy Seat, Fire Thief, and Marta while the party travels through Chaos toward the Blood Plane. No item receipt, chest, or reward.
- **1270 — 血色位面，冤家路窄** The party uses Blood Crystals from earlier Blood Legion drops as navigational media, one per member, to locate the Blood Plane. Zola asks for the killing assignment to grow the spear; no new crystals or spear upgrade are received in this chapter.
- **1271 — 碾压血色位面，圣女之威！** Zola is assigned the Blood Plane extermination and enters battle. The Hemo Ancestor manifests and is struck by a blue attack; the chapter ends before the complete battle result or any reward. No chest opening or item receipt is shown.
