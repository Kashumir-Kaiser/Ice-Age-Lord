## Chapter findings

- **1292:** The first Dragon Cup concludes with Angelina first, Wei Jianyi second, and Ji Xinyue third. The chapter reports ten Top-grade Material Packs, three Legendary proficiency ornaments, and a 30-day 20% building-cost reduction as post-tournament rewards. It does not state who individually received the pack and ornaments; no person is assigned ownership.
- **1293:** Starfire City's forces join Tian Jing against the Death and Sky Ancient Dragons. The fight is still active at the chapter end. Bron's Divine Guard/Return of Causality defensive status becomes permanent through Eternal Power, with the stated voluntary-cancel and low-health conditions. No kill or loot is inferred from this chapter.
- **1294:** The pre-existing Burning Sky Demon Crystal is used in the final attack. Trachet/Zetan is killed and the Eternal Frozen Sea begins thawing. Item rewards are recorded from the explicit first-kill and post-kill notices in Chapter 1295.
- **1295:** Cao Xing is identified as the first survivor to kill a True-God-class Ancient Dragon. The system lists the Frost-Thorn Queen's Frost-Winter Staff and Death Lord's Book (both Upper God artifacts), the Ancient Dragon Codex, Dead Nation: Tomb of Gods, one True-God Silent-Gaze spell scroll, and three free attribute Godheads. World effects include one full-health resurrection after death and improved movement/regeneration in darkness. The system shows Cao Xing leveling to 279 in the captured messages. Cō Lance separately sends a True Darkness Godhead, 158 Trachet/Zetan Death-Condensation Blood Essences, one Eternal-Quiet Scale, and one Death Wing.
- **1296:** Cao Xing's Command skill reaches Legendary after a speech grants 10,000 mastery. He selects three Upper God Godhead types and grants the godheads to Xiao, Binglan, and Elisa; the exact matching is stated later in Chapter 1299. The Harvest Sickle is lent to Cō Lance (existing SX-1476). The 90,000 Divine Power crystals/resources are allocated to support the three breakthroughs; they are not new item drops.
- **1297:** The Ancient Dragon Codex's collection, ability-experience, and projection-summoning functions are inspected. The Death Lord's Book and Frost-Thorn Queen staff are inspected; the book is designated for Tian Jing and the staff for Cao Xing. These are details of Chapter 1295 rewards, not duplicate item receipts.
- **1298:** The previously owned dragon egg hatches into the Light-Dark Dragon pet Diệu Tinh/Ám Tinh. The pet joins the territory. System panels show level 35 and sealed Overlord potential after 450 years of accelerated growth. The egg is not counted as a new receipt.
- **1299:** System notices confirm the mapping and successful godhood: Sword Godhead → Xiao/Sword God; Dragon Godhead → Binglan/Dragon God; Cold-Wind Godhead → Elisa/Cold-Wind Goddess. Each reaches the 300-level cap and gains a unique Lower God skill plus a permanent territory-wide rule benefit.
- **1300:** Liu Muxue transfers five stacks of pooled campaign loot: Wood Crystals ×3,984; Mordema Demon-Vine Fragments ×326,000,000; Parasitic Spores ×58,210,000; Mordema Demon-Vine Magnets ×11,570,000; and Random Magic Seed Bags ×12,460,000. The Wood Crystals convert to 199,200 Divine Power and are handed to Zhala. She begins the True God breakthrough process; success is not confirmed in this batch.
- **1301:** Harold restores the existing Storm Battleaxe (SX-1322) to its complete Upper God Artifact form. Cao Xing binds the Frost-Thorn Queen staff, Botier binds the restored Storm Battleaxe, and Tian Jing binds Death Lord's Book. Their Hewer title gains +500 luck for Cao Xing, +300 for Botier, and +500 for Tian Jing. The axe restoration is a transformation, not a second weapon acquisition.
