## Chapter findings and boundaries

- **Ch892:** Dream-shop exchanges confirm two building blueprints and 45 Divine Power Shards. The blueprints immediately begin upgrades: Mosin Pillar (728h35m) and Lord Temple (965h41m), with no workers or materials needed for these initial steps. The later stock panel reports 395 Divine Power Shards as an aggregate, not an extra receipt. Dream Library outcomes: Binglan learns Dragon God Swordsmanship (seven base skills plus one Legendary and one Half-God skill not yet learned); Andrew returns with Nature's Gift; Xierda awakens the Spider Emperor Web trait; Francesca returns with one-third Eldritch Codex. These are distinct narrative outcomes; only the named necklace and book are recorded as physical item receipts.
- **Ch893:** Xierda uses the already-held Blood Source Godfire and 190 Divine Shards. She had naturally recovered 117 divine power and the system reports 200 points consumed to activate Poison Blood. Her Half-God transformation succeeds and grants “Crimson Abyss · Fatal Kiss.” No new Godfire or shard receipt occurs here.
- **Ch894:** Territory operations report 472 Return Scrolls produced and handed over. A Demi-God Dralamam Light Cannon is completed; its reported damage exceeds 100 million per second for a 10-second beam. Materials for four units are said to be prepared, but three additional cannons are only ordered for completion within a month.
- **Ch895:** The cannon is inspected. Saint Eight remains imprisoned; Mo Yin is still working to break a mental seal, with another half-month estimated. No item receipt, chest opening, or new reward is confirmed.
- **Ch896:** Eldrin Greenleaf and Selena Kasha are coerced into joining as fighters, with their detailed plant and spatial abilities recorded in source context, not as item receipts. No new item or reward receipt.
- **Ch897:** Harold completes and names Night-Rhino King Armor from existing Legendary rhino hide and Night Lionheart Gem; Sarah binds it. Existing enhancement-stone inventory is shown. More than 200 Gold Scurmice stones and one Rainbow Crystal are assigned to the smith, but this chapter does not confirm the exact consumption.
- **Ch898:** Roxie joins at level 151 (population +10, prosperity +1500, Human Fortune +5000, subject to reversal if she leaves or dies). Her exact gift is Divine Power Shards x439. This corrects the preceding approximate spoken amount (“four hundred plus”).
- **Ch899:** Five existing Legendary items are reported at level 150 after enhancement. The total documented Gold Scurmice stone usage is 38. The two items at four stars are Moon Goddess Staff and Starfall Codex; three items at ten stars include Lich Frost Soul Robe, Lich Ice Bone Leggings, and Demon Codex. No Rainbow Crystal consumption is confirmed.
- **Ch900:** The expedition leaves for Eternal Frozen Sea. Church sages seek the Golden Dragon Horn and carry an already-held Church Emblem as an escape item. The horn is not acquired.
- **Ch901:** Eternal Frozen Sea discovery awards exploration XP +500,000 and reputation +1,000. The chapter introduces Ice Ghouls and Icefield Giants, but confirms no item receipt, chest opening, or loot.
