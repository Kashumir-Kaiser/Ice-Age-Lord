# Round 70 Source Note Correction — v7.0.1

This append-only correction fixes the Vietnamese chapter alignment note in the Round 70 audit and catalog.

The original v7.0 note said Cơm Nguội Vietnamese Chapter 1116 matched Chinese printed Chapter 1112. That is incorrect: the Vietnamese title “Thần cấp Băng Sào xuất thế! Bão táp tai kiếp bùng nổ!” matches Chinese printed Chapter 1113, “神级冰巢现世！灾劫狂潮爆发！”. The observed offset at this point is +3.

A second title anchor confirms the same offset: Cơm Nguội Vietnamese Chapter 1127 displays “Bản vẽ Sáng Thế Chi Quang, vũ khí sắc bén đối phó Chân Thần!”, matching Chinese printed Chapter 1124, “创世之光图纸，对付真神的利器！”.

Cơm Nguội requires login to read the chapter body. These two checks use the visible chapter titles only to align editions. The Round 70 reward and event facts come from the Chinese full-text sources, and no Round 70 CSV rows or quantities change.

Do not apply this +3 mapping to other Vietnamese editions without matching titles. Xa Lộ Sách uses a different chapter sequence around this arc; Bách Ngọc Sách has split chapter segments.
