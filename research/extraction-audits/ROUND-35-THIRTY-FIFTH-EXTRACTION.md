# Round 35 — Thirty-fifth extraction (Chapters 762–771)

## Scope

Append-only, source-only evidence from printed Chapters 762–771. The fixed 30-event `EVENT-TIMELINE-v0.6` is unchanged. No source character inventory becomes LL01 starting loot.

## Additions

- **3 item records (SX-0462–SX-0464):** Alia's Legendary Knight of Heavenly Punishment manual; observed use of an already-held Ancient Dragon Scale; conditional 5 kg Legendary Rhinoceros Meat reporting bounty.
- **6 effects (FX35-0001–FX35-0006):** manual skills and learning restriction; existing Stasis Mana Obelisk placement/shield; Life Tree branch growth; Seraphis's single Legendary evolution; Gray Fog King trial terms; Rosie's commission and pending domain.
- **4 observations (OBS35-01–OBS35-04):** Obelisk initialization, unpaid Gray Fog King terms, second-hand fortress capability, Rosie's pending territory grant.
- **1 unresolved conflict:** Ch763 calls the manual `阿丽娅的天罚骑士手册`; Ch769 calls it `阿丽娅的传承之书`.
- **2 structures (STR-0016–STR-0017):** placed existing Obelisk and second-hand report of the Northern Wind Alliance's Silver Radiance Fortress.

## Findings

- **Ch762–763:** Liu Muxue gives Cao Xing the Legendary manual. Two skills are described at Heroic and Legendary grade; its incomplete demigod skill is explicitly unlearnable. Learning requires a knight with honor and faith.
- **Ch763–764:** Gray Fog King agrees to a seven-day Dream Library trial and minimum post-review payment of 80 Divine Power Shards. Cao Xing represents an upfront 50 shards but actually uses 10; the 80-shard reward is unpaid through Ch771.
- **Ch764:** Cao Xing places the existing Legendary Stasis Mana Obelisk between two statues. Placement adds 500 prosperity. It is 32 m tall; its area is worded as “350 square” with unit unspecified; capacity is 1,000,000 mana; its 10 km-diameter dome blocks wind and snow after activation. Effect 3 remains incomplete.
- **Ch765:** one already-owned Life Tree branch is planted and watered with one bowl of Cold Spirit Water. It reaches 1/120 life source and shows 1 point/hour regeneration in this blessed-soil setup; this is not generalized.
- **Ch766:** one bright soul, one Legendary Heart, and an already-held Ancient Dragon Scale are used in Seraphis's observed Legendary evolution. Scale acquisition is unknown here.
- **Ch767:** Silver Radiance Fortress is described second-hand as made from a whole ruin and able to resist Legendary attacks, perhaps even a demigod's arrival. It is not inspected.
- **Ch768:** report notice conditionally offers next-opening priority and 5 kg Legendary Rhinoceros Meat for timely reporting newly found hidden Lost King's City buildings. No claim is recorded through Ch781.
- **Ch769:** manual title variant remains unresolved.
- **Ch770–771:** Rosie completes the C-rank King of Ice Crystal commission and receives 5,000,000 XP. `狂风雪原` remains conditional on her report and is not shown granted by Ch771.
