## Chapter findings

- **1272 — 魔枪进阶，吸收血海本源！** Zola’s previously acquired spear (SX-0938) reaches Lower God Artifact grade after killing Blood Legion forces and absorbing blood-sea essence. The system’s intermediate attack totals are truncated, so no final stat is inferred. System reports large kill/XP outcomes; regular kills may drop XP and small amounts of Blood Crystals, while Legendary Warlords/Blood Generals can drop Legendary Hearts and Blood-Origin Divine Fire.
- **1273 — 魔灵苏醒，泣血魔龙！** After repeated blood-sea absorption, the spear awakens its demon spirit, Blood-Drinking Demon Dragon, and activates Blood Dragon Frenzy and Blood-Soaked Dragon Domain. Further absorption advances a separate spirit-evolution meter, which remains incomplete here. The Hemo Ancestor is exposed and killed; exact receipts are itemized in Ch1274.
- **1274 — 曹星实验，血祖变异** The Filth God’s Heart corrupts the Blood Ancestor into the Upper God Black-Blood God; Marlene Nina kills it. Boss receipts: Black-Blood Godhead (Upper God) ×1, Divine Power Crystals ×542, Blood Crystals ×144,927. Battlefield cleanup after 147 Legendary Blood Warlords and 22 Demigod Blood Generals: Blood-Origin Divine Fire ×18, Legendary Hearts ×84, Commander-grade Blood Warlord souls ×46, Overlord-grade Blood Warlord souls ×17, Overlord-grade Blood General souls ×4, Strongman’s Blood ×1,852, and Blood Crystals ×216,246. The Truth-Eye sage discovers a sealed cache under the Blood Sea Palace ruins: Catastrophe Godhead (Upper God) ×1, Blood-Origin Godhead (Mid God) ×1, Ancient Sword Su Haxin (Semi-Artifact) ×1, and Divine Power fragments ×7,216.
- **1275 — 上古宝剑-苏哈辛，危机！** Details Su Haxin’s curse/disease/mind-control immunity, allied cleansing, 100-km salvation domain, and Reversal: One Sword effects. Its combined Sovereign Decree effect is inactive until it is fused with Magdel; no fusion occurs.
- **1276 — 针对星火城的突袭，清理者降临！** The Cleaners attack Starfire City with gray fog and a True God-level Arrowbearer; allies mobilize. No item receipt.
- **1277 — 清理者—Sapphiron·荆棘犁地！** Defenders confront Sapphiron; battle remains unresolved.
- **1278 — 碾压！星火城巅峰战力！** Marlene Nina and Zola heavily injure Sapphiron; a second Arrowbearer intervenes. No completed reward.
- **1279 — 首杀灭世魔，猎杀勋章！** Sapphiron is killed for the server first. Explicit rewards: Free Forbidden Weapon Chest ×1; Purified Apocalypse Godhead (special) ×1; Free Attribute Godhead (Upper God) ×1; Ancient Red Sky Chest ×1. The chests are not opened; no “Hunting Medal” receipt line was captured, so no medal row is added from the title alone.
- **1280 — 新的上古巨龙，命运巨龙的力量！** Fate Dragon Oruin helps defeat the second True God-level apocalypse demon, Sailer Phose. World rewards repeat Free Attribute Godhead (Upper God) ×1 and Ancient Red Sky Chest ×1, plus Apocalypse Demon Crystals ×12. Sailer’s forbidden weapon, Branch Pruning Shears (修枝剪), is explicitly collected; exact grade/effects remain unknown.
- **1281 — 战后，盟友会议** Post-battle conference sets preparations for the Cleaner invasion. No item receipt or chest opening.
