## Confirmed rewards, items and outcomes

- **Ch912:** First/northern Frost Oath Great Wall battle begins against three half-god ice-nest enemies: Bazaar, the Ice-Nest Tyrant (level 180); Rift Mountain, the Ice-Nest Behemoth (level 175); and Oron, Tzoma's Claw (level 180). No item receipt is shown in this chapter.
- **Ch913:** The A-rank “Ice and Snow's Choice” mission reaches 4/4 battlefronts, perfect rating, and 100%. Base rewards are Cross-Region Teleport Array Blueprint (Legendary) x1, Philosopher's Stone x30, Divinity Crystal x50, and Divine Power Fragment x200. Perfect-rating bonus awards the Giant Warrior title and Jormungand·Ice Abyss Ring (unknown quality). The title panel describes +15% Strength, +20% Armor Penetration, and Giant's Friend (giants deal 50% less damage to bearer; their initial affinity is Friendly).
- **Ch914:** The protagonist drinks Giant Strong Ale. The effect panel states 30,000 HP restored over 60 seconds and +1,000 Strength. This is consumption, not a fresh item acquisition. The chapter also discusses the threat timeline.
- **Ch915:** Meng Yi requests an alliance against Tzoma and offers two half-god church captives, conditional on Mo Yin breaking their mind seals. This is an offer, not a completed transfer.
- **Ch916:** The Jormungand ring is inspected: item level above 100, the source-printed armor range 2431–2252, and 50% chance on hit to grant the ancestor blessing (+10% Strength for 10 seconds). It gives a vague directional clue to the giant ancestor's death location, not recovery of the remains.
- **Ch917:** Immediate cleanup transfer lists Ice Dragon Marks x7,592,034; Glacier Shards x3,678,724; Glacier Cores x112,492; Tainted Essence x3,419,244; Evil Essence x417,928; Rotten Essence x32,185; Dragonrock Ore x166,782; Ice Dragon Crystals (unknown) x42; Coldland Rune Stones (unknown) x38; and Dark Energy Orbs (unknown) x3. More ordinary loot is elided. The source separately states expedition-wide Ice Dragon Marks exceed 20 million and that seven Dark Energy Orbs exist after including earlier battlefronts; those aggregates are not additional receipts. Ice Dragon Crystal's panel states +5% ice affinity and +3% spirit recovery while carried, up to 10 stacks; crushing restores 50 divine power.
- **Ch918:** Revived ancient Icefield Giants accept allegiance after healing. This is recruitment/state change, not an item receipt.
- **Ch919:** 63 giants receive a combined buff sequence within Nesha's Blessing Aura. A Seven-Colored Crystal is discussed as a possible future reinforcement input; none is consumed in this chapter.
- **Ch920:** The protagonist spends 200 Divine Power Fragments each to purify Echo Godfire and Nightmare Godfire, then transfers Ice Dragon Crystals and Divine Power Fragments to Bron and Malenina for their breakthroughs. These are costs and transfers of existing stock, not fresh receipts.
- **Ch921:** Alternate mirror text confirms Bron's half-god breakthrough fails (level -20; all attributes -30% for seven days; Icefield divinity -50%), while Malenina succeeds and becomes a half-god. No item reward is received. The 69shuba page itself remains unavailable.
