# Round 75 — Seventy-Fifth Extraction (Chapters 1162–1171)

Sequential source review of Bqquge Chinese printed chapters 1162–1171, including visible page continuations. This release adds 18 append-only source records (SX-1322–SX-1339); cumulative CSV is 1339 data rows and 69 columns.

## What was recorded

- Ch1162: Harald repairs the original Storm Axe; Cao Xing receives the level-190 Quasi-artifact. Its lack of a core godhead prevents full artifact status. Effects and stats are summarized in SX-1322–SX-1323.
- Ch1163–1164: Imperial shipment and exact altar consumption are retained as distinct rounded/exact figures; 97,164 Hero promotions are displayed. The War God trial awards Heart of Genesis and one War Plane Teleport Stone.
- Ch1165: Heart of Genesis fuses with Bloom's Genesis Shield into the semi-artifact Genesis Shield–Anodite. Shield defenses and Anodite's Embrace are captured separately.
- Ch1166–1168: Abyss invasion preparation, the verified Death Bag/Flute transfer in Ch1167, the Ark's outside support role, and the Church pact are context. The Ark has one Destiny Judgment launcher use remaining.
- Ch1169: Abyss entry grants 50,000,000 Exploration XP and 100,000 Prestige. The Holy Spirit Absolute Seal debuffs and spatial-skill restriction are recorded.
- Ch1170–1171: Starfire reinforcements and continuing Church battle are event/combat context. The battle is unresolved; damage, shield and suppression observations are not converted to loot.

## Vietnamese title checks

Three editions were checked. Cơm Nguội and Hắc Hoàng Đại Đế visibly title local Ch1162 “Nữ thần trong tượng băng, thân phận kinh người!” (capitalization varies); both page bodies are login-gated. Xa Lộ Sách's visible catalog for local Ch1162–1163 instead lists “Điều kiện đột phá Bán Thần, trở về lãnh địa” split across parts (1) and (2). Its local Ch1158 is a different “Lý Niệm Chi Tranh, Bán Thần Chi Chiến” arc. These are edition-specific indexes. They are not used to remap primary Chinese printed chapter numbers or source item mechanics.

## Conflicts and limits

- Ch1163's rounded shipment narration differs slightly from the altar's exact resource cost; both are preserved.
- Ch1163's displayed 97,164 promotion result is retained alongside the stated 120,000 assembled and 25,000 held back.
- Ch1171 ends during combat. No victory or battle reward is claimed.
- No chest drops or physical loot are documented in Chapters 1166–1171 beyond the explicit trial rewards and previously obtained items.
