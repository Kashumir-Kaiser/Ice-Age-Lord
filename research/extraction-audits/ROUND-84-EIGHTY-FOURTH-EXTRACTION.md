## Chapter findings

- **1252 — 十二翼堕天使，寄生丛林之变！** The Sleeping Abyss guardian is a twelve-winged fallen angel and offers a final gift if defeated. No defeat or item drop is shown here.
- **1253 — 传承最终开启！** After three days of killing, the displayed War Blade fragment inventory is **5,487,600**. A later explicit pickup adds **192**; the system then states the **10,000,000**-fragment threshold is complete and prompts synthesis. The fragments visibly combine into the War Blade, revealing the Sunrise King’s Court/War God temple. The visible counts are short by **4,512,208**; intermediate gains are not shown, so the discrepancy is preserved.
- **1254 — 三大神将，最锋利的矛与最坚固的盾！** Clearing War God trial round three yields three explicit rewards: **Olli Hall**, a level-260 Mid God Artifact heavy armor; **Flawless Divine Fire ×3**; and **Divine Power fragments ×1,000**. The armor is discussed as suitable for Bron, but no equip/transfer occurs in this chapter. Its captured stats are recorded in SX-1433.
- **1255 — 第三神将，Edith试炼！** Edith begins the star-fragment resonance trial. No new item receipt.
- **1256 — 月影星劫，最强刺客传承！** Sarah begins Edith’s complete assassin inheritance trial; the outcome is still pending at chapter end.
- **1257 — 执掌星月权柄，超越速度极限！** Sarah wins and inherits Moonlight Astral Calamity. The system records +1,000% base stats, divine-grade proficiencies, territory prosperity and human fate +50,000 each, permanent territory night/moonlight buffs, a level cap of 300, Moonlight Body, Astral Tribulation Divine Domain, and named skills. These are progression/mechanics, not item receipts.
- **1258 — 万盾之主，凯拉的试炼！** Sarah explicitly transfers to Cao Xing the **Star-Moon Mark**, received after defeating Edith. It is the key to Edith’s Gate and the next Keira trial. The Keira challenge begins.
- **1259 — 逆天的真理之瞳，破关！** The party uses the Truth Eye to analyze Keira’s defense trial. No new item receipt.
- **1260 — 传承礼物，万盾真解！** Keira presents the Heavenly Defense Godhead and Myriad Shields True Interpretation as final gifts. The explicit handover is in the following chapter, so this chapter alone is not logged as receipt.
- **1261 — “你好，昔日的女武神。”** The two items fly into Cao Xing’s hands and the system confirms receipt of the **Upper God Heavenly Defense Godhead** and **unknown-grade Myriad Shields True Interpretation**. The party enters the Sunrise Temple and begins the ancient Valkyrie’s final trial.
