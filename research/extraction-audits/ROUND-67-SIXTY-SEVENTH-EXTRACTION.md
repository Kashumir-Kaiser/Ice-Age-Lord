# Round 67 — Sixty-Seventh Extraction (Chapters 1082–1091)

Sequential source review, research-only. None of this becomes LL01 T0 inventory, a guaranteed new-game reward, or a change to the fixed event calendar.

## Sources and cross-checks

- Primary readable chapter text for the extraction: Bq8xsw, printed Chapters 1080–1091. Relevant continuation pages were followed for item, acquisition, transformation, and chapter-boundary passages; this is a source ledger, not a full-text transcription. Bq8xsw is a repost mirror; it contains malformed character substitutions and truncation markers, so extracted quantities and item names are taken only from readable system/shop text.
- Independent chapter-directory cross-check: Bqquge, https://www.bqquge.org/851 . It lists the same printed sequence through Ch1091. Earlier Round 66 chapters were read there; this pass also checked the Bq8xsw continuation text for direct comparison.
- Catalog/title cross-checks: Qidian (https://www.qidian.com/book/1040133596/), Hongxiu (https://www.hongxiu.com/bookquery/qbqbjdtuosdy), XS8 (https://www.xs8.cn/bookquery/pegtwzxazfvx), and Bq8xsw’s own chapter directory (https://www.bq8xsw.com/chapterlist/110975.html). They agree on the chapter sequence and titles for the battle and rewards arc; these listings are not treated as separate item receipts.
- Vietnamese editions/catalogs located: Cơm Nguội Novel, “Tận Thế Băng Phong: Ta Xây Dựng Hoàn Mỹ Lãnh Địa” (https://comnguoinovel.com/truyen/tan-the-bang-phong-ta-xay-dung-hoan-my-lanh-dia), and Bách Ngọc Sách’s “Băng Phong Tận Thế: Ta Chế Tạo Hoàn Mỹ Lãnh Địa” (https://bachngocsach.me/truyen/doc-quyen-dich-bang-phong-tan-the-ta-che-tao-hoan-my-lanh-dia). Cơm Nguội’s directory reports 1,358 chapters, but its chapter body did not render in the available browser view. Its title index is offset by one around this range (Vietnamese Ch1082 is titled as the battle start, while Chinese printed Ch1082 is the teleport chapter and the battle starts in Ch1083). Bách Ngọc Sách / TruyenYY expose thousands of translated slices whose numbering is not one-to-one with the printed Chinese chapters. Xá Lộ Sách (https://xalosach.com/bang-phong-tan-the-ta-che-tao-hoan-my-lanh-dia-0.html) stops at original Ch962. These Vietnamese sources help identify title variants and mirror availability, but were not used to assert Ch1082–1091 receipt quantities.

## Chapter and event findings

- Ch1082–1085: all survivors are teleported to Mirror Territories; the first battle day begins at 06:00 and the protective shield rises at 22:00. Battle text mentions drops, but quantified system receipts arrive at the beginning of printed Ch1086 after the pooled first-day spoils are gathered. Do not back-attribute that receipt block to Ch1085.
- Ch1086: the seven readable system receipt lines are pooled spoil from Cao Xing's field forces. The book count line is cut off after the item name. No omitted quantity or other hidden lines are invented.
- Ch1087: the Ch1080–1081 shop offer becomes a confirmed redemption. One selected relic was chosen from 108 listed options. Tong's 200 Divine Power Fragments and Elissa's Legendary Heart are transfers from prior stock, not new receipts.
- Ch1088: Elissa consumes/uses the previously logged selected relic and Legendary Heart to break through; Tong consumes the previously logged Divine Fire plus 200 previously held fragments. No second receipt for these uses.
- Ch1089: the communication blueprint is an explicit breakthrough reward.
- Ch1090: Malenina's Legendary gear fusion yields the named quasi-artifact set. No separate Legendary input is counted as a new receipt here.
- Ch1091: discusses the new form and prior battle rewards; the actual leaderboard result is in Ch1092, outside this batch. Ranking rewards remain unclaimed at this cutoff.
- The fixed 30-event schedule is unchanged. This chapter-derived battle timing and shop evidence remains research, not a retroactive edit to that schedule.

## Conflicts preserved

- Ch1080 lists the four Legendary armor prices as 1.0, 1.2, 1.0, and 1.2 yi marks (4.4 yi total), and five weapon prices as 1.5, 1.2, 1.4, 1.3, and 1.5 yi (6.9 yi total). That itemized offer total is 11.3 yi; Ch1087's batch confirmation says 10.3 yi for all nine. Both source statements are retained; per-item price totals are not silently changed.
- Ch1086's market balance rises from 2.4 yi to 52.4 yi (net 50 yi), while Ch1087 lists purchases of 10.3 yi + 31.7 yi + 10 yi + 0.5 yi = 52.5 yi and says the mark balance is exhausted. The 0.1 yi mismatch remains unresolved.
- Several Ch1080 set listings give both per-piece and full-set prices that do not reconcile arithmetically (for example, four listed gold set pieces at 0.3m each against a 1.0m full-set price). The ledger preserves each displayed number and marks piece count unknown where the text does not state it.
- Earlier Round 66's audit table omitted SX-0958 from the Ch1078 receipt range even though the v6.6 CSV already contains twelve Ch1078 rows (SX-0958–SX-0969). This is an audit-table range correction, not a CSV row change. Ch1073's Deep Sea Breath Concoction receipt total 251 also conflicts with the Ch1074 inventory breakdown totaling 162; both remain visible in v6.7's source lineage and neither is overwritten.
