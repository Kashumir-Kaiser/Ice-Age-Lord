# Round 74 — Seventy-Fourth Extraction (Chapters 1152–1161)

Sequential source review; research-only. Extracted campaign items, event rules, observed winnings, and narrative abilities are not LL01 starting inventory, guaranteed new-game loot, or changes to the fixed calendar.

## Sources and method

- Read Bqquge's Chinese primary text for printed Chapters 1152–1161, following visible page and next-chapter links for each chapter.
- Cross-checked Vietnamese catalog/title metadata across [Cơm Nguội](https://comnguoinovel.com/truyen/tan-the-bang-phong-ta-xay-dung-hoan-my-lanh-dia), [Hắc Hoàng Đại Đế](https://hachoangdaide.online/tan-the-bang-phong-ta-xay-dung-hoan-my-lanh-dia), and [Xa Lộ Sách](https://xalosach.com/bang-phong-tan-the-ta-che-tao-hoan-my-lanh-dia-0.html).
- Cơm Nguội and Hắc Hoàng Ch1158 both show the Life Tree resurrection / World Tree mystery heading, which maps by story anchor to Chinese printed Ch1155. Xa Lộ Sách's catalog Ch1158 is “Lý Niệm Chi Tranh, Bán Thần Chi Chiến (1),” a different arc. Numbering and chapter segmentation vary materially; do not align by raw number.
- Cơm Nguội's inspected body page requires login. Hắc Hoàng states that only the first 100 chapters are readable without login. Xa Lộ Sách supplied visible catalog titles. No gated body text was used, no login/app restriction was bypassed, and Vietnamese catalog metadata was not used to infer quantities.

## Limits and contradictions

- The Chapter 1152 Bronze Claw label conflicts with the Chapter 1150 rank table. Both source statements remain visible in the ledger; no score correction is inferred.
- Race pool, payout and organizer share are reported for one observed event only. Bets and winnings belonging to other participants are not protagonist receipts.
- Chapter 1155 says remaining plant-heart stock was consumed but does not provide a full count. The input totals are left unspecified.
- Qingling's two heart inputs are simultaneous; the 300-year acceleration is attributed to the pair, not separately to each heart.
- Zola remains frozen; the method and timing for revival are unknown.
- Chapter 1161's trade document remains a proposal pending Imperial review. The Storm Axe's historical identity is not treated as an acquisition or a confirmed transfer.
- Vietnamese catalog numbers differ by edition and may point to different story arcs. Vietnamese body gates were respected; catalog titles support only source alignment.
- These entries remain research candidates. They do not alter the fixed T0 world, inventory, event schedule, or guarantee future chest/arena rewards.
