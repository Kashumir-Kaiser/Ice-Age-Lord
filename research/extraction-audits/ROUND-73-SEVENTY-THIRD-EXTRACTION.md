# Round 73 — Seventy-Third Extraction (Chapters 1142–1151)

Sequential source review; research-only. None of these campaign rewards, items, chest results, or event rules become LL01 starting inventory, guaranteed new-game loot, or a change to the fixed calendar.

## Sources and method

- Read Bqquge's Chinese primary text for printed Chapters 1142–1151 through each visible continuation. Cross-checked chapter titles/order against the separate [Bq8xsw chapter directory](https://www.bq8xsw.com/chapterlist/110975.html); Ch1142's title matches.
- Checked multiple Vietnamese editions: [Cơm Nguội](https://comnguoinovel.com/truyen/tan-the-bang-phong-ta-xay-dung-hoan-my-lanh-dia), [Hắc Hoàng Đại Đế](https://hachoangdaide.online/tan-the-bang-phong-ta-xay-dung-hoan-my-lanh-dia), [Xa Lộ Sách](https://xalosach.com/bang-phong-tan-the-ta-che-tao-hoan-my-lanh-dia-0.html), and the [Bách Ngọc Sách catalog](https://bachngocsach.me/truyen/doc-quyen-dich-bang-phong-tan-the-ta-che-tao-hoan-my-lanh-dia).
- Cơm Nguội and Hắc Hoàng Ch1142 both show “Pháp bào cấp Thần Khí, trời đất biến chuyển dữ dội!” while Chinese print Ch1142 is “Four-Symbol Dragon Lord, versatile Dragon Soul Envoy.” Xa Lộ Sách segmented Ch1142 says “Legendary weapon enhancement.” These are different edition sequences; align by story title/anchor, never raw number.
- Cơm Nguội and Hắc Hoàng chapter bodies require login; Xa Lộ Sách displays only a short preview and gates the rest behind its app. No gated body was used, no login or app restriction was bypassed, and Vietnamese pages supply no reward quantities.

## Limits and contradictions

- Shop prices are recorded as offers only. The unknown dragon egg was not purchased in this range.
- Guild upgrade resource totals from levels 11–20 are incomplete in the narrative; no aggregate spend is calculated.
- Only four of eight Epic Guild Chest outputs are named in the visible reward list. No full loot table is inferred.
- Individual Bright-Moon box outputs are partly pooled across participants. Only source-explicit recipients are named in the ledger; no unnamed drop rows or recipient guesses are added.
- Observed Red Sky and Bright-Moon chest results are not general drop guarantees.
- Vietnamese chapter access is limited to visible title/index metadata for this review; their numbering diverges from the Chinese printed sequence.
