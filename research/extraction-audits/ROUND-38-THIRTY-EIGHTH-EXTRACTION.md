# Round 38 — Thirty-eighth extraction (Chapters 792–801)

## Scope

Append-only, source-only evidence from printed Chapters 792–801. The fixed 30-event `EVENT-TIMELINE-v0.6` remains unchanged. Batch chest outputs are recorded as observed outcomes, not as guaranteed loot. The Church's demand at the end of Ch801 is unresolved.

## Additions

- **13 item records (SX-0486–SX-0498):** selected Ancient Moon and Gold Chest rewards; three ancient elixir recipes; Jade Elephant Hearts; Type VII Decryption Chip; Life Spring Essence.
- **12 effect records (FX38-0001–FX38-0012):** missile, Symbiotic Scorpion, dragon armor, tree growth, chip use, three recipes, radar/armor and two short-form item effects, and the crafted potion.
- **9 observations (OBS38-01–OBS38-09):** chest batch, upgrade outcomes, crystal harvest, tree growth, elixir yield discrepancy, two Dream Library outcomes, and the pending Church encounter.
- **No new event schedule entries, source structures, or route observations.**

## Findings

- **Ch792:** Renis becomes a Legendary Sky Dome Hunter and receives the passive *Sky Dome Eye*. One observed batch opens 183 Silver, 48 Gold, and 15 Moon ancient chests. Chests yield named equipment and recipes, but the exact chest-to-item map and probabilities are not given.
- **Ch793:** the Moon batch includes one Ancient Intercontinental Missile, one Symbiotic Scorpion, one Dragon-class Power Armor, three Jade Elephant Hearts, one Type VII Decryption Chip, and three named epic recipes. The missile's listed impact radius is 100–500 km and maximum range 20,000 km. The Scorpion's armor shred has a 10,000 HP-per-hit cost. The armor has standby/combat/automatic modes. The three potion recipes have distinct effects and stated ingredient lists.
- **Ch794–795:** the chip resets the Frost-Core Eternal Silence Hammer's binding; Harold binds it. The Hammer's existing legendary effect is enhanced. Five other legendary equipment pieces receive separate upgrade results; these aren't a reusable guaranteed upgrade table. Crystal people deliver 174 Dark Crystals, 15 Flash Crystals and 2 Rainbow Crystals; Cao Xing gives them 5,000 Shadow Purple Crystals.
- **Ch796:** three Jade Elephant Hearts accelerate the Pale Sacred Tree by 500 years, to 800, and its combat XP blessing becomes +80%.
- **Ch796–797:** for 99 Life Tree leaves, the witch says she produced 90 Life Spring Essence bottles with more than 20 failures; the later system receipt says 63 bottles. Keep both counts and do not infer a yield rate. A bottle restores 60% HP to Legendary beings and 100% below Legendary, then heals 250,000 HP per second for 100 seconds; cooldown 10 minutes.
- **Ch798–799:** Purifying Light partially reduces the source character's curse; full removal is not established here. Gray Fog King demonstrates *Ashen Raven's Embrace* after the Dream Library; Ch799's 100-shard payment is corrected in Ch800 to 150.
- **Ch800–801:** Hya receives Dream Library access and later pays 200 shards in Ch801. Her nine-orchid domain lasts briefly each day without significant cost and boosts her while controlling vines. An Oracle Great Sage approaches the territory, uses a Holy Light Wash that clears abnormal conditions, and asks entry; Cao Xing refuses. The Church demands Isabelle's return. No transfer or confrontation result is shown by Ch801.
