# Round 69 Extraction Audit — Chapters 1102–1111

## Scope and method

Extracted printed Chapters 1102–1111 from the Ice Dragon War / Phase II transition. Mapped printed chapter numbers through the Bq8xsw directory rather than assuming consecutive page IDs. Cross-checked key receipts, transactions, shop purchases, and Phase II reward rules against Bqquge chapter text. Searched Vietnamese catalogs on Cơm Nguội Novel and Bách Ngọc Sách, plus Vietnamese title results; neither yielded readable text that could be reliably aligned to this printed chapter range. Their catalog and numbering limitations are documented below.

This is source research only. Rows are not LL01 starting inventory, guaranteed new-game loot, or active fixed-calendar event grants.

## Interpretation controls

- The 5,000,000-mark Ch1103 first-phase reward is shop redemption quota, not deposited currency.
- Ch1104's two meat products are market offers; no unsold inventory is claimed as sold.
- Ch1105's relics and fighter scrolls are acquired and promptly opened/used; later narrative effects do not duplicate the receipts.
- Ch1109 lists ten hidden shop offers. Only five purchased spell scrolls are acquisitions; other stock remains offer-only.
- The Doomah Frostblood Crystal reward is distinct from the Ice Dragon Crystal.
- Existing aggregate balances and earlier quota values are context only, not new receipts.
