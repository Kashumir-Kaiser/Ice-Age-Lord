## Findings and transaction boundaries

- **Ch772–773:** the second wave opens 100,000 Lord slots, filled within five seconds; Ch773 describes several hundred thousand arrivals including leaders and elite residents. A black jar is bartered to Cao Xing for 1 kg Legendary Rhinoceros Meat. A mercenary captain buys a Large Energy Cannon for 30m Krona. Cao Xing sells a mixed lot of 1,178 stones, gems, and fragments (723 fragments) to Leonard for 480m; individual contents are not itemized. An unnamed ancient weapon is only said to exceed 100m value; no sale is recorded.
- **Ch773–776:** the underground auction hall has three floors, with a stage on the first and ring corridors/private boxes above. Cao Xing receives 100 Life Tree Leaves for 330m gross, gets 66m cashback, and pays 264m net. Hunter Dragon Poison starts at 20m and sells for 90m to a Hero-grade buyer. Oar Pearl sells to another buyer for 30m.
- **Ch776–777:** Twisted Vine Seeds and Star-Star Magic Beans cost 57m together; their individual prices and batch sizes are unknown. Cao Xing buys a 45.12 kg Ancient Ambergris block for 200m and gets 40m cashback. Frost Elf's Condensed Tear and Dragon Blood Amber cost 100m together; split unknown. Cao Xing consumes the tear and retains the amber.
- **Ch778–780:** Cao Xing buys the Crimson Broken Blade for 8bn and the Frost-Core Hammer for 100bn. The Thousand Blades manual sells for 6bn and Vime's Epic Tower Shield for 22bn to other buyers.
- **Ch774 and Ch779:** Leonard's direct 20-bottle Force-Magic Elixir deal closes for 420m. Separately, 100 bottles sell at auction for 30bn to the buyer in third-floor box 11. The 100-bottle lot's starting price differs between chapters (1bn versus 10bn). Keep the 20-bottle transfer and 420m payment but do not infer whether the consumed sample is a twenty-first bottle or a different unit valuation.
- **Ch774 and Ch781:** Cao Xing consigns two complete Hero-tier golems. Ch781 identifies the first/left PAC-7000: start 10 Divine Power Shards, 1-shard increments, current high bid 100. The auction remains open with no transfer shown. Preserve the earlier 50-shard quote with unclear scope.
